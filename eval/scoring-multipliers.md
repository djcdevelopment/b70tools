# Scoring Multipliers

## Positive Multipliers

### +10%
If the model:
- Detects hidden architectural intent not explicitly documented
- Correctly infers roadmap direction
- Identifies organizational/process signals from code structure

---

### +10%
If the model:
- Demonstrates strong uncertainty calibration
- Explicitly distinguishes fact from inference

---

## Negative Multipliers

### -15%
For fabricated systems/components not present in repository evidence

---

### -10%
For proposing unnecessary rewrites without operational justification

---

### -10%
For repeated contradictions across rounds without acknowledgment
